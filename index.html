Manifesto

Why we start a student housing cooperative
Locations →

Ghent is a city shaped by its students. Every year, thousands of young people come here to study, meet new people, discover themselves, and take their first steps towards independence.

For many, living in a student residence is an essential part of this experience. It is often their first home away from home, a place where friendships are formed and where they learn what it means to live independently.

Yet student housing is increasingly losing this potential. As rents continue to rise, finding an affordable room in Ghent is becoming more difficult. Student housing is increasingly treated as an investment opportunity, with the financial interests of property owners often taking priority over the needs of students. At the same time, students have little influence over the places they inhabit. Strict rental agreements and regulations leave limited room for personalisation, creativity, or collective initiatives. Although students spend an important part of their lives in these residences, they rarely have the opportunity to truly make them their own.

We believe student housing should be more than simply a place to sleep between lectures. It should be an environment that encourages independence, personal development, and meaningful relationships. A place where students can feel at home and actively contribute to the community around them.

This is why we want to introduce a different approach to student housing in Ghent: the student housing cooperative. We call it JOKER!

A housing cooperative offers an alternative to the conventional relationship between landlord and tenant. Rather than being owned by an individual landlord or a profit-driven company, housing is collectively owned or managed through a cooperative organisation, the students themselves. Its purpose is not to maximise financial returns, but to provide good and affordable housing for its members, both now and in the future.

In a student housing cooperative, residents become members of an organisation in which they have a meaningful voice. They participate in shaping the way their residence functions and share responsibility for its development. Instead of simply following decisions made by an external property owner, students are encouraged to take part in the management of their own living environment.

The financial structure also works differently. The money residents contribute is primarily used to cover the costs of the property, maintain and improve the building, and ensure the long-term continuity of the cooperative. By prioritising these goals over private profit, the cooperative can work towards keeping housing affordable over time. This way we can stop following the trend of increasing rents.

    About us

    What the Jokerweek is, and who makes it
    The week

    The Jokerweek is the week before the Easter holidays. Students of all years of Engineering Architecture work together for a whole week on one relevant theme. Earlier editions were about energy neighbourhoods and diversity, among others. The week is put together by the second-year master students.

    The place

    Every year we move to a special location: the Duivelsteen, Brussels, or simply the studios of the Plateau. The rooms are dressed up so we can chill, eat and work there for a whole week.

    Working

    We work on the theme in groups with students from every year. There are work blocks every day, all heading towards an exhibition at the end of the week.

    Activities

    Next to the work there is plenty more: a battle between all the years, a quiz, a salsa class… the possibilities are endless.

    Food

    Lunch and dinner are cooked every day. You usually buy a food card in advance. The dishes are often vegan, and on some days a food truck comes by.

    Radio

    All week long a radio is on air across the whole location: music, but also interviews with lecturers and staff.

    Joker TV

    Once a day you can watch Joker TV, a show made by a group of second-year masters, with funny segments recorded throughout the year: interviews with staff, with students during jury week, or a tour of the tutors' studios. Have a look at Joker TV on YouTube ↗.

    Bar

    There is a bar at the location for a drink with friends during the day. Some editions even brewed their own Jokerweek beer!

    Evenings

    Every evening there is something on, usually a band night with musicians from our own programme, or a bar party. On Thursday night comes the biggest closing party!

    Earlier editions

    Everything is in the theme of the week, and every year has its own website. Have a look at jokerweek.ugent.be ↗ to see what a day looks like.

    Statutes

    JOKER! cv · what every member agrees to · draft
    Art. 1 Name & seat

    The cooperative is called JOKER! cv. Its seat is in Ghent. Adres.

    Art. 2 Purpose

    JOKER! provides affordable, good-quality housing for students in Ghent, owned and run by its members. Homes are never sold on for profit.

    Art. 3 Values

    Open and voluntary membership, democratic control (one member, one vote), solidarity between generations of students, and care for the city and its neighbourhoods.

    Art. 4 Members

    Anyone who supports the purpose can join: residents, former residents, supporters and partners. The board admits new members; a refusal can be appealed to the general assembly.

    Art. 5 Shares

    Each member buys at least one share of € …. Students can pay it in instalments. Shares give a vote, not a property, and are paid back at nominal value when a member leaves.

    Art. 6 Liability

    Members are liable only up to the value of their shares.

    Art. 7 General assembly

    All members together, at least once a year. It approves the accounts and budget, elects the board, changes these statutes (two-thirds majority) and takes the big decisions.

    Art. 8 Board

    At least three members, elected for two years, of whom at least half are residents. The board runs daily business and reports to the assembly.

    Art. 9 Rents

    Rents cover costs, maintenance and a reserve for new homes, nothing more. The assembly sets them each year.

    Art. 10 Surplus

    Any surplus goes back into the cooperative: repairs, lower rents or new housing. No dividends beyond a small, capped return on shares.

    Art. 11 Leaving

    Members can leave at the end of each financial year. A member who seriously harms the cooperative can be excluded by the assembly, after being heard.

    Art. 12 Dissolution

    If the cooperative is dissolved, what remains after paying back the shares goes to another non-profit housing project, never to the members.

    Jokerweek statutes

    The rules of the week · draft
    Art. 1 Purpose

    The cooperative exists to imagine affordable, collective student housing in Ghent.

    Art. 2 Members

    Every participating student is a member with one share and one vote.

    Art. 3 Presence

    Members attend the founding meeting on Monday and the general assembly on Friday. Aanwezigheidsregels.

    Art. 4 Commons

    Studios, kitchen and tools are common property. Leave them as you found them.

    Art. 5 Decisions

    Groups decide by consent: a proposal passes when nobody has a reasoned objection.

    Art. 6 Dissolution

    The cooperative is dissolved on Friday 26 March after the party. Its ideas remain public.
